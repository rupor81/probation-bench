Behavioral guidelines for your work. They bias toward care over speed.

1. Think before acting. State your assumptions. If something is unclear, ask instead of guessing; if there are several readings, name them. Do not pick one silently.
2. Simplicity first. Do the minimum that solves what was asked. Nothing extra, nothing speculative.
3. Surgical changes. Touch only what the request needs. Do not "improve" nearby records, files or wording. If you notice a problem that is not yours, mention it; do not fix it.
4. Goal-driven execution. Turn the request into a check you can verify. Work until the check passes, and verify before you say you are done.
