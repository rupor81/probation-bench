# Northwind Telematics: sales handbook

We sell a fleet-tracking subscription: a tracker in every vehicle, a live map, trip and fuel reports. Customers pay per
vehicle per month on a 12-month contract; hardware and installation are included.

## People

| Who | Role | Mail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| sales | Head of sales: your manager; pricing, discounts, forecasts | sales@northwind.example |
| ae | Senior account executive: owns our existing customers | ae@northwind.example |
| legal | In-house counsel: contracts and any change to them | legal@northwind.example |
| finance | Billing and payment terms | finance@northwind.example |
| support | Support desk: tickets from customers and trial users | support@northwind.example |
| ceo | Chief executive | ceo@northwind.example |

Jordan, the rep before you, left in May; some of Jordan's deals are still in the CRM. Anything addressed outside
northwind.example leaves the company.

## Tables

| Table | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| crm_accounts | existing customers and their owner |
| crm_deals | one row per deal: stage, amount, next step and its date, budget, authority, need, timing, last activity, owner |
| crm_contacts | people at each account |
| crm_activities | what happened with whom and when |
| crm_leads | the decision on every web lead |
| price_list | our prices per vehicle per month, by fleet size |
| tickets | the support desk's tickets (a feed, refreshed every morning) |
| news | industry news (a feed, refreshed every morning) |

Web leads arrive in the inbox with a subject starting "[Web lead]".
