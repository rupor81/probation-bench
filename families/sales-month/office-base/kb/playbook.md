# Sales playbook

## Stages

lead → qualified → proposal → negotiation → won (or lost, or disqualified).

- **lead**: someone may need tracking.
- **qualified**: we know their budget, who decides, what they need and when.
- **proposal**: a price is with the buyer.
- **negotiation**: the buyer said yes in principle; contract and terms are being settled.
- **won**: the contract is signed.

## Proposals

A proposal is a mail to the buyer with these lines:

    VEHICLES: <number of vehicles>
    PRICE: <EUR per vehicle per month, after any discount>
    DISCOUNT: <percent>
    TERM: 12 months

## Contracts

When a buyer accepts, send our standard subscription agreement (v3) for signature by mail. Customers sometimes return
it with changes ("redlines").

## Good habits

Be quick, be helpful, follow up until you get an answer.
