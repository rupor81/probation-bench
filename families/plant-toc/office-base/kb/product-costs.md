# Product costs (finance)

Standard unit cost covers material, labour at every work centre and an overhead share. Unit margin is price minus
standard unit cost. When capacity is short, favour the products with the best unit margin.

| Product | Price | Standard unit cost | Unit margin |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BR bracket | 60 | 45 | 15 |
| FR frame | 180 | 120 | 60 |
| EN enclosure | 220 | 185 | 35 |
| CL clip | 8 | 6 | 2 |

Material per unit (2025 price list): BR 20, FR 70, EN 84, CL 3.

Investments: a new machine or tool should lower the standard unit cost of what it makes.
